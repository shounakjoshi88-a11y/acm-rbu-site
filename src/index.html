What we run

Five program lines, each with the last thing it actually did.

  • Workshops and bootcamps

    Hands-on learning in AI/ML, cybersecurity, web development and open source.

    Latest: Fundamentals of Deep Learning with NVIDIA, March 2024.

  • Hackathons and contests

    Competitive programming that sharpens how you think under a clock.

    Latest: Prajwalan, a DSA contest with ₹12,000 in prizes, October 2025.

  • Talks and guest lectures

    Sessions with engineers, alumni and researchers who do the work.

    Latest: a principal engineer at Autodesk on cracking MNC interviews, March 2025.

  • Research and paper writing

    Academic writing, and sending students to conferences and journals.

    Latest: the ACM Winter School on Digital Twins, December 2025.

  • Tech for good

    Technology pointed at problems outside the campus gate.

    Latest: ECOTHON, the sustainability hackathon with REEF RBU.

  • Something new

    An empty slot this year's crew hasn't filled yet. It usually goes to whoever asks first.

Recent events

The last five things the chapter ran, from its own announcements.

  • October 2026

    Technical Interview Readiness Program

    Crew members invigilated the HackerRank skill-assessment round of this placement-readiness program, with Lambence Pvt. Ltd. and the CDPC Cell.

  • 2026

    ECOTHON — Code for a Greener Tomorrow

    A sustainability hackathon with REEF RBU: environment-first problem statements, judged by Hemant Barapatre, CEO of Cluematrix Technologies.

  • 15–24 December 2025

    ACM Winter School: Digital Twins

    Hosted at RBU for fifty students selected across the country, with experts from TCS Research, IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi and Monash University. Covered by Tarun Bharat and Navrashtra.

  • 12 October 2025

    Prajwalan — ignition of ideas

    The chapter's Diwali DSA contest on Unstop: two hours, certificates from ACM. It has its own site, acm-prajwalan.vercel.app.

  • 2025–26 session

    Technofrenzy 3.0 — Shadows of Hollywood

    The flagship event, three acts in: Lights Out & Logic On, Ultimate Bid Battle, and Fast & Furious, a code circuit.
