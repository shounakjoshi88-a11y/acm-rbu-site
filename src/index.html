The manifest

“To inspire and empower students to become leaders in the field of computer science and information technology through exposure to cutting-edge research, hands-on learning experiences, and community engagement.”

  • Promote interest in computer science and allied disciplines.
  • Encourage student-led technical events, workshops and hackathons.
  • Bridge the gap between academia and industry through expert talks and panel discussions.
  • Provide platforms for collaborative learning, coding competitions and project development.
  • Foster the ethical and responsible use of technology.

Vision and objectives as compiled in Ramdeobaba University's professional-chapters record.

plate 03 · modules

Working modules

The chapter runs on five program lines, straight from the university record. Each plate notes that line's most recent run.

  • wl-1

    Workshops & bootcamps

    Hands-on learning in AI/ML, cybersecurity, web development and open source. Most recent: Fundamentals of Deep Learning with NVIDIA, March 2024.

  • wl-2

    Hackathons & contests

    Competitive programming that sharpens problem-solving. Most recent: Prajwalan, the DSA contest with ₹12,000 in prizes, October 2025.

  • wl-3

    Tech talks & guest lectures

    Sessions with industry professionals, alumni and researchers. Most recent: a principal engineer at Autodesk on cracking MNC interviews, March 2025.

  • wl-4

    Research & paper writing

    Academic writing and participation in conferences and journals. Most recent: the ACM Winter School on Digital Twins, December 2025.

  • wl-5

    Tech for good

    Technology pointed at real community problems. Most recent: ECOTHON, the sustainability hackathon with REEF RBU.

  • wl-6

    Open slot

    Reserved for something this year's crew hasn't thought of yet.

plate 04 · transmissions

Recent transmissions

Pulled straight off the chapter's announcement feed — the last few things the machine ran.

  • October 2026

    Technical Interview Readiness Program

    Crew members invigilated the HackerRank skill-assessment round of this placement-readiness program, in association with Lambence Pvt. Ltd. and the CDPC Cell.

  • 2026

    ECOTHON — Code for a Greener Tomorrow

    A sustainability-focused hackathon with REEF RBU: environment-centric problem statements, judged by Hemant Barapatre, CEO of Cluematrix Technologies.

  • 15–24 Dec 2025

    ACM Winter School: Digital Twins

    Hosted at RBU for fifty students selected across the country, with experts from TCS Research, IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi and Monash University. Featured in Tarun Bharat and Navrashtra.

  • 12 Oct 2025

    Prajwalan — ignition of ideas

    The chapter's Diwali DSA contest on Unstop: two hours, gold/silver/bronze certificates from ACM. Its own site: acm-prajwalan.vercel.app.

  • 2025–26 session

    Technofrenzy 3.0 — Shadows of Hollywood

    The flagship event, three acts in: Lights Out & Logic On, Ultimate Bid Battle, and Fast & Furious — a code circuit.
